TunWork is a recruitment platform designed to connect candidates
with companies.

Candidates can create an account and build their professional CV.

Candidates can search for job offers based on different criteria.

Candidates can apply for available job offers.

Companies can create an account and publish job offers.

Companies can manage their published offers.

Companies can view statistics related to their job offers.

TunWork also provides AI-based assistance for recruitment and
communication between candidates and companies.
